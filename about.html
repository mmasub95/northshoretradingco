How We Got Here

The North Shore Origin Story

It started the way most good things do — around a kitchen table, ripping packs and talking about how there was no real card shop left on the island that treated collectors with respect.

Nassau County is home to hundreds of thousands of TCG players. Weekend tournaments, school lunch trades, family hobby nights — cards are woven into the fabric of Long Island life. But finding a dedicated shop with real inventory, knowledgeable staff, and a community vibe? That was harder than pulling a Rainbow Rare.

"We wanted a shop where you could walk in on a Tuesday afternoon, ask about the meta, and leave with exactly what you needed — or just hang out and talk cards."

— North Shore Trading Co. Founders

So we built it. North Shore Trading Co. opened on Willis Ave in Albertson with a clear mission: stock what players actually need, price fairly, buy collections honestly, and run events that bring the community together.

We're constantly restocking singles, bringing in new sealed product every week, and running live break events both in-store and on stream. This is just the beginning.

2022 — The Beginning
North Shore Trading Co. Opens Its Doors
We opened at 1042 Willis Ave, Albertson with our founding inventory of Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh, and MTG singles and sealed product.
2022 — First Events
Weekly Break Events Launch
Our first live break events sell out instantly. We begin streaming on Whatnot and YouTube, bringing the North Shore community together online and in-store.
2023 — Growth
Singles Database Grows to 10K+
Collectors start coming from all over Long Island for our singles selection. We add a dedicated display case for graded PSA and CGC cards.
2025 — Community
Expanding the Event Calendar
Multiple break formats every week. New arrival drops promoted in advance. A card shop that feels like home for Long Island's TCG community.
Now
Long Island's Card Shop
Still growing. Still adding inventory. Still hosting events every week. Come see what we've built — we'd love to have you in the shop.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🤝
Fair Pricing. Always.
We price singles based on real market data — TCGPlayer, eBay sales, trends. No gouging, no mystery markups. What you see is what you pay.
💬
Real Knowledge
Every team member plays the games we sell. Ask us anything — set legality, grading grades, meta picks. We'll give you an honest answer, not a sales pitch.
🏡
Community First
We're neighbors. We sponsor local tournaments, host events for all ages, and make sure the shop feels welcoming whether you're 8 or 48.
💰
Honest Buyouts
When we buy your collection, we explain every offer. You'll know exactly how we calculated it and what each card is worth — no lowball surprises.
🚚
Safe Shipping
Every card shipped gets a toploader, sleeve, and bubble mailer at minimum. Singles get extra care. We package like we'd want our own cards shipped.
🔄
Fresh Inventory
New product drops weekly. We don't sit on old stock — we keep the cases current, rotate in new singles, and restock sealed product constantly.
